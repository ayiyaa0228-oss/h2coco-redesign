FAQs

Is H2coco Australian? +

H2coco is proudly 100% Australian owned and operated. Our head office is based in Surry Hills, NSW.

Where does H2coco source its coconut water from? +

Our products are sourced from Thailand & Vietnam. We work closely with all of our suppliers to ensure we guarantee Australian quality on all of our products. We only work with suppliers who are governed by a strong code of conduct, ensuring all employees are treated and paid fairly and who have sustainable guidelines in place to minimise their environmental impact.

Do your products contain any added sugars? +

No, our entire range of beverages are 100% natural and preservative free.

Are your products made from concentrate? +

Our products are NOT made from concentrate. Our drinks are all 100% natural and bottled at the source for freshness and purity.
